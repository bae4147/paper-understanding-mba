Case 1
Transformational Leadership & Work Engagement in Remote Settings
Give me a summary of the uploaded article

This article examines the relationship between transformational leadership and work engagement in remote work settings, focusing on the role of a supervisor's digital communication skills as a moderator.

Context (Research Setting)

The study was conducted with 410 consultants in Italy who worked remotely during the Covid-19 pandemic, exploring how leadership dynamics function in a fully remote environment.

Intervention (What Was Tested)

The researchers tested a moderated mediation model examining how transformational leadership influences work engagement through perceived supervisor support, and how supervisors' digital communication skills affect this relationship.

Mechanism (Why It Worked)

  • Transformational leaders can engage remote workers by providing support and motivation, even at a distance.
  • Supervisors' digital communication skills enhance their psychological presence, crucial for reducing feelings of isolation.

Outcome (Results)

  • Transformational leadership → perceived supervisor support (+)
  • Perceived supervisor support → work engagement (+)
  • Supervisors' digital skills moderate this relationship

Takeaway

Developing supervisors' digital communication skills is essential to effectively support and engage remote employees.

Case 2
Discovering Your Authentic Leadership
Please summarize the paper

This article explores the concept of authentic leadership and how individuals can develop their leadership abilities through self-awareness and personal growth.

Context (Research Setting)

The research was based on interviews with 125 leaders from diverse backgrounds — including CEOs and mid-career leaders — who shared their life stories, personal struggles, and transformative experiences.

Intervention (What Was Tested)

The authors investigated how individuals could become authentic leaders by:

  • Framing their life stories to gain self-awareness
  • Practicing their values and principles consistently
  • Balancing intrinsic and extrinsic motivations
  • Building strong support teams

Mechanism (Why It Worked)

  • Authentic leaders emerge from their unique life stories, which shape their leadership styles
  • Self-awareness is crucial — leaders need to understand their motivations and values
  • Authentic leaders practice their values even under pressure

Outcome (Results)

Authentic leadership leads to:

  • More effective leadership as individuals inspire and empower others
  • Sustainable long-term results in organizations
  • A deeper sense of fulfillment and connection to purpose

Takeaway

Authentic leadership is not about imitating others but about understanding and embracing one's own story and values.
